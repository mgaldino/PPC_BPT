The puzzle is why the right-wing coalition (UDI/RN), which held a Senate majority, accepted this progressive reform after blocking similar initiatives in 1990, 1995, 1998, and 2001.

- H_EA (Equity Appeal): Lagos's public equity challenge, in the context of high issue-salience during a presidential campaign, drove the right to accept the reform out of electoral fear.

- H_CC (Core Constituency): The right accepted because its elite base no longer valued the 57 bis subsidy (its material value had declined over time).

Their BPT analysis produced a posterior of approximately 1.0 for H_EA (98 dB vs H_CC, 110 dB vs H_MV).
