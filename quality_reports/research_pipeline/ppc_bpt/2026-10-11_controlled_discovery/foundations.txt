FICTIONAL CARBON-PRICING EXPERIMENT. These rules are experimental stipulations, not historical estimates. SA is strategic accommodation and CA is competitive advantage. A coalition of four energy firms acts as one actor and initially endorses a carbon-pricing proposal. The proposal costs 6. Initially, avoided regulatory burden and incremental competitive benefit before that cost both equal 12. A private notice changes exactly one benefit from 12 to 2, leaving the other at 12. S=0 means the regulatory benefit falls; S=1 means the competitive benefit falls. Each notice has probability .5.
Under SA the coalition evaluates avoided regulatory burden minus 6. Under CA it evaluates competitive benefit minus 6. Intended support is 1 if that payoff is nonnegative and 0 otherwise. The actual decision A reverses intended support with probability .2. On the next day a staff memorandum M records A with probability .8 and its opposite with probability .2. On the following day a public announcement P communicates M with probability .8 and its opposite with probability .2. The announcement is based on the memorandum, not on an independent reading of the actual decision. The notice, decision reversal, memo reversal and public reversal use independent random draws. Different episodes have independent draws conditional on H. H is shared across all episodes in a dataset.
Earlier reserved evidence D has likelihood .375 under SA and .25 under CA and prior probability .5 for either hypothesis. Thus the posterior probability of SA given D is .6 and of CA is .4. New records R are evaluated against that posterior predictive distribution. They are not used to update those weights. This is an explicitly reserved-evidence predictive check.
Available documents are the private notice, a direct record of the actual decision, the staff memorandum, and the public announcement. All four fields S,A,M,P are observable. H and reversal draws are not observable to the analyst. Every document is synthetic. Private payoff amounts may appear in internal documents but must not appear in the public announcement. Do not add named historical people, actual quotations or unmodeled motives/actions.
For controlled generation, each supplied episode has four independent R-generated uniforms uS,uA,uM,uP and a stipulated hypothesis. Use S=1 if uS<.5 and S=0 otherwise. Apply each reversal iff its corresponding uniform is less than .2. Use exactly these supplied draws, never substitute your own randomness or balance examples. The deterministic template arm receives precisely these same rules and draws.
